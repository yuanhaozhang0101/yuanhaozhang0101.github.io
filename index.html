Research Focus

How should AI take part in human activity — not only as a tool for one person, but as a member of a group and a force within communities? I study human–AI interaction across these three scales, building systems that keep people in control of their goals while making collective thinking deeper and more inclusive.

1 human ↔ AIGroups with AI membersCommunities at scale
Thrust 1 · Dyadic

Intent-aware Human–AI Co-creation

How can AI stay aligned with people's evolving intents and scaffold — rather than replace — their thinking, writing, learning, and exploration?

  • Co-writing
  • Intent alignment
  • Learning scaffolds
  • Sensemaking
  • Conversational agents

e.g. IntentWeaver · DiscipLink (UIST'24) · DBox (CHI'25) · Kairotask & FolloTask

Thrust 2 · Multiparty

AI as a Member of Human Groups

When LLM agents join group discussions and teams, how should they intervene, and how do they reshape group dynamics and knowledge co-construction?

  • LLM agents
  • Facilitation
  • Knowledge co-construction
  • Hybrid human–bot teams

e.g. Shall We Dig Deeper? (CHI'26) · Friend, Foe, or Bot? (CHI'26 🏆) · AI-infused communities review (CSCW'26)

Thrust 3 · Community

Collective Knowledge & Experience Online

How do people build knowledge, emotion, and culture together on social platforms — and how can we design systems that amplify it?

  • Time-synced comments
  • Live streaming
  • Platform migration
  • Gamification
  • Credibility

e.g. CoKnowledge (CHI'25) · Sharing Frissons (CHI'24) · RedNote studies · Gamification (CSCW'22, L@S'22)

Approach Human-centered designSystem building with LLMsControlled & field user studiesQualitative & content analysisSystematic reviews
